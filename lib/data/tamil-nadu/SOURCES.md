# Tamil Nadu map sources

## Assembly constituencies

- Files: `tn-acs.json`, `tn-districts.json`
- Constituency geometry: DataMeet Community Maps Project, India Assembly Constituencies (2008 delimitation), CC BY 2.5 India.
- Repository: https://github.com/datameet/maps/tree/master/assembly-constituencies
- State outline: svg-maps by Victor Cazanave, CC BY 4.0.
- Processing: constituency polygons are bundled locally and dissolved into the 38 survey districts. Coordinates are rounded and no boundaries were drawn or inferred.

## Use

- Nothing is fetched at runtime. The polygons are community maps, not certified legal boundaries.
- Public credit: "Map: DataMeet, CC BY 2.5 IN · Outline: svg-maps, CC BY 4.0".
