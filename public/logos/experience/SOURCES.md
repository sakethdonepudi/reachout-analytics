## Deliberately omitted

The source material also names **Benz**, **Mestek** and **Shriram** in the
training list. Those names do not identify a single legal entity or verified
mark with enough confidence for this grid, so no substitute artwork has been
published.

## Usage

All marks remain the property of their respective owners. The supplied assets
are displayed without recolouring, inversion or redrawing. Logos sit on a
neutral light tile so their original brand colours remain legible in both site
themes.
